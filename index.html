Our Story

A heritage of taste, tradition & warm hospitality

Welcome to Mama's Cafe

Established in 2050 BS, Mama's Cafe has been a beloved landmark along the Arniko Highway in Banepa. For generations, we've been serving travelers, hikers, and locals with our signature Khuwa specialties — a rich, traditional milk product that forms the heart of our unique menu.

Recently shifted to a new and improved location, we offer a cozy, welcoming atmosphere where you can enjoy freshly made Khuwa Toast, rich Khuwa Lassi, energizing Khuwa Shake, and our special Khuwa Peda sweets.

Whether you're stopping by after a long hike, waiting for friends, or simply craving authentic local flavors, Mama's Cafe is your perfect retreat. Easy parking, friendly staff, and unforgettable taste — that's our promise!
